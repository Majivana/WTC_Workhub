# Reporting

The implementation includes student progress and dashboards, a supervisor queue with pending
reviews and attendance exceptions, open escalations, and a filtered CSV work-summary export.
Available report coverage is narrower than the full planned operational set: richer campus/role,
evidence-status, and payroll-oriented reporting requires further requirements and review.

CSV export is the administrative export currently implemented. It escapes values and supports
optional user, work-period, and status filters. Business access rules still apply.
